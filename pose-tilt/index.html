Protocol and limitations

The same simulated input is rigidly rotated about the normalized object origin around +X. Direct inference uses the tilted XYZ with no pose correction, no rescaling, and the same grid. Its mesh is rotated back only for scoring.

The known-angle control reuses the upright prediction and displays it at the known tilt. Its scores are the upright scores: this is not pose estimation, new hardware contact, or a validation benchmark.

CD is the sum of two mean nearest-surface distances in normalized coordinates. F@0.02 uses a distance tolerance of 0.02. The CAD is used locally for scoring and is not published with this test.
